Privacy Policy

CloudDrive2 uses Google Drive APIs to provide cloud storage management features.

The application accesses only the Google Drive data authorized by the user.

Authorized data may include file names, folder names, metadata and content necessary for file operations.

Collected data is used solely for providing file browsing, uploading, downloading and synchronization services.

No user data is sold or disclosed to third parties.

Users may revoke access at any time through their Google Account permissions settings.
